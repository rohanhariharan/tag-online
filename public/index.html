TAG

A side-scrolling game of tag. Jump between floating platforms, grab powerups, and pass the tag on by running into someone. Whoever is still it when the clock runs out loses — 2–4 players, each on their own network.

How to play

  • Move A / D or arrow keys, or the on-screen stick.
  • Jump Space / W — hold for height, tap for a short hop.
  • Dash Shift — a burst in the air or on the ground.
  • Tag run into anyone while you're it.
  • Powerups grab speed, a shield or a double jump.
  • Win don't be it when the timer ends.

Powerups

  • Speedrun 45% faster for 8s
  • Shieldblocks one tag
  • Double jumpone extra jump in the air

Maps

    Runs on an authoritative server, so a laggy player can't teleport through walls. Everything is yours to host: one Node process, no database, no third-party calls.
